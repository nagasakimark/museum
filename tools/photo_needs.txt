PHOTOS STILL NEEDED — museum exhibits
============================================
Each line: exhibit (the English name used in the museum) → what to photograph.
Key used in tools/photo_reject.txt / photo_overrides.json is  room-id|exhibit name.

Tags:
  [icon]   an idea, not a thing — a photo won't help; keep the drawn icon
  [draw]   better drawn in the app (I can make these) than photographed
  [©]      the artwork/design is still under copyright — a photo of it can't be used
  [person] the exhibit is about a real person — use the suggested object/place instead
  Anything showing people: no recognisable faces (crowds far away, hands only, from behind).

Summary: 76 rejected + 1346 without a photo. Of those 1346, about 1186 need a real photo; 129 are better as icons, 9 better drawn, 18 copyrighted, 4 about people.
